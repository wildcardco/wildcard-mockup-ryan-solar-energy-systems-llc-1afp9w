Solar education

Understand it before you buy it.

We'd rather explain solar than sell it. Here's the short version — and when you call, we'll gladly give you the long one, in plain English.

  • Panels turn Indiana sunlight into DC electricity — even on cloudy days.
  • An inverter converts it to the AC power your home, barn, or shop runs on.
  • Extra power flows back to your utility or into batteries for later.
  • Your meter and your bill both start running the right direction.
